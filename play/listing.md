# Store listing

**App name:** Tile Timer

**Short description (80 characters at most):**

One-tap timers in Quick Settings. Start one from the lock screen, no unlocking.

**Full description:**

Tile Timer puts your timers in the Quick Settings panel. Swipe down from the top of the screen, even on the lock screen, and tap a tile. That's it.

• Up to eight tiles, each with its own time, down to the second
• A Custom tile that lets you pick any time, right from the lock screen
• The countdown stays in your notifications with +1 min and Cancel
• Rings through silent mode at your alarm volume, vibrates, and shows a big Stop button over the lock screen
• Run several timers at once; each tile is independent
• Matches your phone's colors
• Free. No ads, no account, no internet, no data collected.

Long-press a tile to open settings.
